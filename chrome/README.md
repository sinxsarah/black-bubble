# Black Bubble 0.3.17 for Chrome

Black Bubble is an independent project. It isn’t made, approved or supported by OpenAI, ElevenLabs or Fish Audio.

## Install

1. Type `chrome://extensions` into Chrome’s address bar and press Enter. Turn on **Developer mode** at the top right and leave it on. If you turn it off later, Chrome may switch the extension off.
2. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the folder that contains `manifest.json`. If you downloaded a ZIP, unzip it first; the folder you want is the one named `chrome` inside it. Keep that folder on your computer.
3. Refresh your ChatGPT tab. Look for **0.3.17** beside **Talk hands-free** in your new or existing chat. If you already loaded this same folder, click the reload arrow on its extension card first.

## Talk

Click **Talk hands-free** once. Keep the tab open in front of you. Stop talking for about two and a half seconds and it sends. After the reply plays, the bubble listens again. The square ends the call. Use the diagonal arrows for a smaller bubble so you can see the chat.

The waveform opens the controls. Reply voices include ChatGPT’s own Read aloud, a voice on your device, ElevenLabs and Fish Audio. Fish Audio hasn’t been tested by us yet. The last two send the reply to the provider you choose. Chrome’s speech recognizer handles dictation, and Chrome may send your voice to Google’s speech service to turn it into words.

## TROUBLESHOOTING

- **Paused before you spoke?** Go back to the chat and look in the message box at the bottom. Send or clear the draft and any attachments, then start again.
- **Still listening after you stop?** Try a quiet room or headphones. The microphone hears the room, and a second voice can keep your turn open.
- **Telling a long story?** There’s no two-minute cutoff. If Chrome restarts listening, your words carry on. If it loses the microphone or stops returning words, the bubble leaves what it heard in the message box for you to edit and send.
